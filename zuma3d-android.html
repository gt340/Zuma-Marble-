3D ZUMA

Match three or more glowing marbles before the chain reaches the skull.

Best campaign0Best endless0Levels0/10

Campaign

Clear every marble to finish a level. Stars are earned from your play score.

How to play

  • Aim by dragging; the dotted guide shows exactly where your marble lands. Release to shoot. (Mouse: move to aim, click to shoot.)
  • Match 3+ touching marbles of one colour. Marbles inserted between two groups can join both.
  • When a match leaves a gap, the front part stops. If the colours at the gap match, it rolls back and chains a combo.
  • Swap the current and next marble (tap them, or Space). Think before you shoot: where you insert matters.
  • Marbles with a symbol give you a power-up when destroyed: Bomb (blast radius), Rainbow (becomes the best colour), Slow, Reverse. Store up to 2 of each and arm them with the buttons (keys 1–4).
  • If the chain reaches the skull you lose a life. Watch for the red warning!
  • Keyboard: arrows aim, Enter shoots, Space swaps, P / Esc pauses.
